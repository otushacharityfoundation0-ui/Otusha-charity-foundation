About Otusha Foundation

Committed to transparency, compliance, and real impact.

Driven by Integrity & Compassion

Otusha Charity Foundation is a non-governmental organization (NGO) committed to uplifting communities through comprehensive humanitarian aid, educational advancement, and entrepreneurship development.

Established in the year 2010 with a vision of creating a world where every individual has the opportunity to thrive, the foundation operates with a set of core values centered on compassion, collaboration, integrity, empowerment, and innovation. This comprehensive blueprint outlines the foundation's mission, vision, core values, objectives, and implementation strategies, aimed at making a sustainable impact on the lives of individuals and communities in Nigeria and beyond.
